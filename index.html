Who we are

Real Return is a performance marketing agency. In this policy "we", "us" and "Real Return" mean Real Return Marketing Limited, a company registered in England and Wales with company number 14776451, whose registered office is Suite 103 Westgate House, 44 Hale Road, Hale, Cheshire, WA14 2EX.

If you have a question about this policy or your information, email daniel@realreturnmarketing.co.uk or call 0161 960 0084.

What this policy covers

  • our website, realreturnmarketing.co.uk
  • enquiries, calls and emails with us
  • the marketing accounts our clients ask us to run or report on

Information we collect

When you enquire. Your first and last name, email address, company website, approximate revenue and advertising spend, and anything you write in your message. If you book a call, Calendly passes us your name, email and the time you chose.

When you become a client. Contact details for the people we work with, invoices and the records of our work together.

When we run your marketing accounts. With the access you give us to platforms such as Meta, Google Ads, Shopify and Klaviyo, we see the advertising, email and sales data in those accounts and use it only to run and report on your marketing. Where those records include your own customers' details, you are the controller and we process them on your behalf under our agreement with you.

How we use it, and our lawful basis

What we do Why the law allows it
Reply to your enquiry and assess whether we are a good fit Our legitimate interest in answering people who contact us, and steps you asked for before a contract
Deliver our services and run your marketing accounts To perform our contract with you
Keep financial records Legal obligation

We do not use your information for automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the service providers that help us run our business, such as our website hosting, email and calendar tools. Each is bound to protect it and to use it only on our instructions. We may also share information if the law requires it.

We never sell personal information.

International transfers

Some of our service providers store data outside the UK. Where they do, we rely on the UK's adequacy regulations or on the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum to keep it protected to UK standards.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not become work: up to 2 years from our last contact, then deleted.
  • Client records and invoices: for as long as we work together, then 6 years for tax and accounting.
  • Access to your marketing accounts: for as long as we work together. You can remove our access in each platform at any time, or ask us to.

How we protect it

We keep your information on reputable, access-controlled services, limit it to the people who need it for their work, and keep each client's information separate.

Cookies

Our website does not use advertising or analytics cookies, so no consent banner is needed.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of your information
  • correct it
  • delete it
  • restrict or object to how we use it
  • send it to you or another organisation in a reusable format

Email daniel@realreturnmarketing.co.uk. We will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113. We would appreciate the chance to put it right first.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top.
